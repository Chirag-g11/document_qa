# Product and Customer Support Policy

## Returns and refunds
Eligible physical products may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. Final-sale items and customized products are not eligible for return unless they arrive damaged or defective.

## Refund processing
After an approved return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing usually takes 5–7 business days after approval. The time for the funds to appear can vary by payment provider.

## Warranty
Most electronic products include a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage, unauthorized repairs, and normal wear and tear are excluded. Customers should retain their proof of purchase to make a warranty claim.

## Customer support
Support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. local business time. Customers should provide their order number and a short description of the issue when contacting support.
