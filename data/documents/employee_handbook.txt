EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — DEMONSTRATION CORPUS

Leave policy:
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted to the manager at least five working days in advance. Emergency leave should be reported to the manager as soon as practical.

Remote work:
Employees may request remote work for up to two days per week, subject to manager approval and team requirements. Certain roles that require on-site equipment may not be eligible for remote work.

Learning and development:
Employees can request reimbursement for approved job-related courses. The course must be approved by the manager before enrollment. Reimbursement requires proof of payment and successful completion.

Working hours:
The standard working day is eight hours, excluding the meal break. Team-specific schedules may differ, and employees should follow the schedule communicated by their manager.
